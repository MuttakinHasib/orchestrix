Indigo is for intent. The primary button is the only solid accent fill on a screen. Focus rings, selection and automation marks use it as a line. Status colors mean status. Success, error, warning and info appear only on execution states, validation and alerts, as text on a soft tint. Everything else is ink. Three text weights and two hairlines carry the hierarchy.
